Introduction to the Cercle Celtique de Rennes

The Cercle Celtique de Rennes is a non-profit organization under French law of 1901, based in Rennes, whose mission is to promote and preserve the intangible cultural heritage of Brittany. Through its diverse projects, it fosters artistic and cultural exchanges. Through its cultural outreach and educational activities in neighborhoods, throughout the region, and in schools, it plays a significant role in Rennes' cultural landscape.

The Cercle Celtique de Rennes offers a wide range of activities and workshops, both individual and group-based, ranging from traditional Breton dance to music, as well as singing, embroidery, and the Breton and Galician languages. The association's activities give rise to numerous projects both internally and externally, such as the Sevenadur festival and cultural discovery workshops, thus reflecting the richness and diversity of Breton heritage.
